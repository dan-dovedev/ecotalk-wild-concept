# Ecotalk Wild

A concept by Dan Jones for Ecotalk. Not a live Ecotalk product.

**Open it:** https://dan-dovedev.github.io/ecotalk-wild-concept/

Two ideas behind one link:

- **Members' app** (`app.html`): tablet and phone. NatureCams, stories from Bowyers Wood and Downicary, Spot it, the shareholder vote, Impact, and the plan and eSIM one tap away. On an iPad or iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen to use it full screen.
- **Business portal** (`business.html`): laptop first. SIMs and people, eSIM for new starters, usage and one monthly invoice with every SIM itemised, then Impact & ESG (company land restored, a nature impact certificate, an impact statement) and team Wild Points. Switch between a 12-SIM and a 148-SIM fictional company to see the same features at both sizes.

## Real and concept

Facts about Ecotalk's reserves, beavers, species, NatureCams, prices and eSIM timings come from ecotalk.co.uk, Ecotalk customer service and press coverage, October 2026. Points, levels, votes, events, sample members, the two fictional companies, company "m² restored" figures, business prices (shown as indicative), the ESG certificate and everything marked "Idea" are concepts, and are labelled as such on screen.

Photos and footage are Ecotalk's own, used to illustrate. No accounts, payments or data collection: everything runs in the browser.
