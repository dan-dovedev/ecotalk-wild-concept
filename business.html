Suggestions for the business team

More ways to make the land count

Ideas · not live
🪧

Adopt a patch

Name a hectare at Bowyers or Downicary after the company, with a yearly survey of what's moved in. A visit, a sign and a story for the all-hands.

🥾

Team volunteer days

Planting and survey days at the reserves, booked from here. Counts towards CSR and wellbeing hours, and gives staff a reason to care.

🏆

Team Wild Points

Staff earn points in the members' app for sightings and challenges. Teams and sites compete, and winners pick where the next tree goes.

⚡

Energy + mobile, one green account

Ecotricity business energy and Ecotalk mobile together, with one combined impact report. The natural route through the Ecotricity business team.

♻️

Old phones in

Collect retired handsets for refurbishment or recycling and log the numbers in the ESG pack.

🎥

Quarterly reserve update

A short video from the reserves for company meetings, so the impact feels real, not just a number on a certificate.

📊

Scope 3 data, done properly

Mobile emissions data for supplier reporting, using a method agreed with Ecotalk before any figures are published. No guesswork.

🤝

Refer a business

When one Ecotricity business customer refers another, both get extra land restored in their name.

🏛️

Tender-ready wording

Pre-written answers for public-sector social value and environmental questions, backed by the figures above.
